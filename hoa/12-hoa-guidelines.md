# HOA — Granite Vista Community Association

**Source:** Granite Vista Homeowners Association — Community Association Rules and Design Guidelines, revised 9/1/2018. Managed by City Property Management Company (Phoenix, AZ).

> Reference summary only. The full guideline document governs; consult it and the Architectural Committee before any change. One section references "City of Goodyear," likely a template artifact; the property is in Waddell.

## Rules most relevant to this house
- **HVAC:** no roof-mounted A/C units; no window A/C units.
- **Gates:** any gate not installed by the builder requires approval; no gates to common area / adjacent property.
- **Garage doors:** kept closed except during use (up to 1 ft open for ventilation allowed).

## Accessory structures (rear yard only, committee approval required)
- Play structures: ≤10 ft high; deck/platform ≤4 ft; ≥7 ft from perimeter wall.
- Ramadas/gazebos: ≤120 sq ft under roof; ≤10 ft high; ≥7 ft from perimeter wall; match house color; matching roof tile.
- Storage sheds: ≤8 ft high; ≥7 ft rear setback; materials in harmony with the home.

## Landscaping
- Front yard installed by builder; any change requires approval.
- Turf: only Cynodon dactylon 'Mid Iron' hybrid Bermuda sod (or approved artificial turf with ≥7-yr warranty; 2×2 ft sample required).
- Decomposed granite: Stage Coach Brown, 1/2 in screened, 2 in depth.
- Artificial turf perimeter: 6×6 in concrete or brick border.
- Permitted plants: listed in the guideline's Exhibit B (extensive desert-appropriate list).
- No change to natural lot grading.

## Pools & spas
- No above-ground pools (hot tubs/spas excepted).
- Equipment concealed from view and placed to minimize noise.
- Backwash water contained on the lot.
- Rear wrought-iron pool fencing: neutral earth tone to match home/existing fencing.
- City pool-fence rules apply; submit fencing with pool plans.

## Driveways, lighting, windows, signs
- Driveway extensions ≤50% of lot width; prior approval; earth-tone coatings only.
- Builder light fixtures may be replaced (not relocated) without approval; security lights after 10 p.m. must be motion-activated; front/side bulbs ≤60W; ground/path low-voltage ≤20W; no low-pressure sodium.
- No window awnings; permanent window treatments on front windows within 90 days; no foil/sheets/newspaper coverings.
- Sunscreens/tint require approval; gray, charcoal, brown, or beige only.

## RV parking
- Allowed in a designated RV garage, or in an approved, screened backyard (not visible from neighbors/street).
- No parking on gravel, granite, or landscaping.
- 48-hour limit for load/unload on driveway or street.

## Architectural request & enforcement
- Submit Exhibit A (Architectural Request) to City Property Management; acknowledgment within 5 business days or the request is deemed denied; review within 30 days.
- Approval is not a warranty of code compliance and does not replace required permits.
- Fines: $10–$500 per day (per Arizona Revised Statutes §33-1803).
