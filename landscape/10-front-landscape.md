# Front-Yard Landscape

**Source:** Advance Landscape Design "Boulder Package" (Granite Vista 5500's), dated 7/21/2021. This is the builder-installed front-yard package.

## Package materials
- 2 × 15-gallon trees (single staked)
- 20 × 5-gallon shrubs
- 2 boulders (~100 lb each)
- 1/2 in screened Stage Coach Brown granite, ~2 in depth
- Pre-emergent herbicide over the granite area
- Soil mounding (on-site soil)
- Automatic irrigation: 4-station timer, pressure vacuum breaker, one drip valve
