# EV Charging

**Source:** Smart Charge America service estimate, signed 12/16/2024. Installer: Smart Charge America / Live SMART Charge (ROC# 327937). Utility: APS.

## Vehicle & charger
- Vehicle: 2024 Tesla Model S
- Onboard charger: single 48A (48A max)
- EVSE: Tesla Mobile Connector (customer-supplied)
- Receptacle: Bryant NEMA 14-50R

## Circuit (as installed)
- Breaker: 2-pole 50A in the main panel
- Conductors: three 90 ft #8 THHN copper + one 90 ft #10 THHN copper (ground)
- Conduit: 90 ft of 3/4 in EMT
- Continuous draw: 32A (Tesla Mobile Connector limit on a 14-50)
- Estimated charge rate: ~27 miles of range per hour
- Location: rear interior wall of the 2-car garage, directly behind the main panel
- Labeled: "car charging only"
